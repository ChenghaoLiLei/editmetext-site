EditMeText has no internet connection, no account and collects no data. Your files exist only where you choose to keep them.

What is stored

The text you open or create exists in memory only while you edit it; saving writes it to the location you pick in the system file picker (on this iPhone, iCloud Drive or another file provider). To protect against loss, the app keeps a local draft of what you are editing a few seconds after you stop typing, and deletes it automatically once the file has been written. Recently opened files are remembered only as a system bookmark used to open them again; their content is not copied.

What leaves your phone

Only what you do yourself: saving to a location you choose such as iCloud Drive, or sharing a TXT or PDF with the system share sheet. The app itself makes no network requests. There is no server and no account.

Format conversion

Converting PDF, Word, Pages, RTF, HTML and OpenDocument files to plain text happens entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded and no third party is involved. The original file is never modified.

In-app purchase

The Full Version is a one-time purchase handled by Apple’s App Store. We only know whether this device has purchased it; we never see your name, payment method or any personal information. Restoring a purchase also goes through Apple only.

What we do not do

No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs. No access to contacts, photos or location. No AI services. No tracking.

Permissions used

Files: to open and save the files you choose. Access is limited to the exact file or folder you selected. No other system permission is required.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes the local drafts and the recent-documents list. Content you saved to files stays where it is and is not affected. You can also clear the recent-documents list in Settings at any time.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect information from children.
